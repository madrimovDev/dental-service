# Klinika dasturlari tartibni emas, ishni avtomatlashtirmoqda

2024–2026 yillarda stomatologiya va klinika boshqaruv dasturlarida ekran tuzilishi deyarli o'zgarmadi. Kalendar hamon har kreslo uchun alohida ustunli vertikal vaqt to'ri. Statuslar kartochkadagi kichik belgi va alohida "kutish zali" ro'yxati orqali ko'rsatiladi. O'zgargani shu tartib ustiga qo'yilgan avtomatika: ovozli parodont xaritasi, ambient AI yozuvchi (scribe), rentgen ustidagi AI belgilar, tasdiqlash va recall "agent"lari, ma'lumotdan oddiy tilda so'rash. Foydalanuvchilar funksiya chuqurligidan ko'ra tez o'rganishni va hamma narsa bitta oynada bo'lishini maqtaydi. Eng ko'p shikoyatlar: sekinlik, ortiqcha kliklar, ish vaqtidagi yangilanishlar, chalkash hisobot va billing, bulut uzilganda ishning to'xtashi. Ilmiy dalil bu shikoyatlarni og'irroq qiladi: shifokorlar EHR'ni o'rtacha **SUS 45,9** ball bilan baholaydi, har bir SUS ballning yuqoriligi burnout ehtimolining taxminan 3% pastligi bilan bog'liq, dental charting tizimlarida yangi foydalanuvchilar vazifalarning atigi **16–64%** ini to'g'ri bajargan, ogohlantirishlarni darajalarga ajratgan shifoxonada eng jiddiy ogohlantirishni qabul qilish ajratmaganidagi **34% ga qarshi 100%** bo'lgan. MDH va O'zbekistonda bozor IDENT, 1C, StomX va MacDent qo'lida, ularning zaif nuqtasi administrator ish joyi, mahalliy kontekstni esa Telegram, naqd pul va 2025-yildan majburiy QQS belgilaydi. Dental OS uchun xulosa: to'rt yangi ekran kerakli yo'nalishda. Ular ustunlik berishi uchun ogohlantirishlar darajalarga ajratilishi, holat ma'lumoti saqlanishi, oflayn ishonchlilik, Telegram'ning birinchi darajali kanal bo'lishi va laboratoriya buyurtmasida majburiy maydonlar zarur.

## Tartib barqaror, innovatsiya avtomatlashtirishda

Bu bo'limdagi manbalar, boshqacha belgilanmagan bo'lsa, vendor materiallari: press-relizlar, yordam sahifalari va mahsulot sahifalari. Yordam sahifalari interfeys qanday ishlashini ishonchli ko'rsatadi, natija raqamlari esa tekshirilmagan marketing. Kalendar bo'yicha bozor bir konvensiyaga kelishgan. Open Dental'da kun ko'rinishi **har operatoriya (kreslo) uchun bitta ustun**, vertikal vaqt o'qi va gorizontal "hozirgi vaqt" chizig'idan iborat. Bajarilgan qabullar kulrang, buzilganlari ustidan chizilgan. Yon panelda "kunlik ishlab chiqarish va kunlik maqsad" ko'rsatiladi. Uchrashuvlar "Pinboard"ga sudrab tashlanib boshqa kunga ko'chiriladi, ASAP va Unscheduled ro'yxatlari ham shu yerda ([Open Dental Manual](https://opendental.com/manual/appointments.html)). Bu manba sanasiz yordam sahifasi bo'lib, 2024-yildan oldingi interfeysni tasvirlashi mumkin. UK'dagi Dentally statusni kartochkaning yuqori o'ng burchagidagi kichik belgi bilan ko'rsatadi: Pending → Confirmed → Arrived → In Surgery → Completed. Belgi bosilganda keyingi bosqichga o'tadi ([Dentally Help](https://help.dentally.com/en/articles/6709513-what-a-patient-s-appointment-status-means)). "Arrived" bemorni virtual kutish zaliga o'tkazadi va taymerni ishga tushiradi. Rang kechikish darajasini bildiradi: **yashil = erta, to'q sariq = 5 daqiqagacha kechikkan, qizil = 5 daqiqadan ortiq kechikkan** ([Dentally Help: Waiting Room](https://help.dentally.com/en/articles/3548877-waiting-room)). Dentally'da status allaqachon ish oqimini ishga tushiruvchi trigger hamdir: "Completed" bosilganda to'lov hisoblanadi, smeta, rozilik shakli va keyingi qabul yaratiladi.

2024–2026 yillarda kalendarning ko'rinishi emas, bo'sh joylarni avtomatik to'ldirish o'zgardi. Curve 2025-yilda "Smart Fill"ni yangiladi: bo'sh slotlarni tez chaqiriladigan bemorlar bilan protsedura qiymati bo'yicha saralab to'ldiradi ([Curve blog](https://curvedental.com/blog/thankful-for-curve-users)). Planet DDS 2026-yil iyulda ishga tushirgan **DentalOS**da Confirmation Agent va Recall Agent ishlaydi. Ular qabullarni o'zi tasdiqlaydi va jadvalni yangilaydi ([Planet DDS](https://www.planetdds.com/newsroom/dentalos-enterprise-platform/), vendor). Yangi joylashuvni tasvirlagan biror vendor manbasi topilmadi.

Klinik ish joyidagi eng katta siljish **ovoz**. Henry Schein One 2026-yil 30-aprelda Dentrix Ascend uchun Voice Notes (kreslo yonidagi suhbatdan tuzilgan klinik yozuv), Voice Perio, Image Verify va Detect AI'ni e'lon qildi. Detect AI'ning tavsifida "clinicians make the final call" deyilgan ([Henry Schein One](https://www.henryscheinone.com/about-us/press-release/dentrix-ascend-introduces-next-generation-clinical-workflow-built-with-aws/)). Oddiy yozuv **5 daqiqa** bilan cheklangan, ambient rejimi esa cheklanmagan va qimmatroq tarifda ([Dentrix Ascend Learn](https://learn.dentrixascend.com/dentrix-ascend-voice-notes/)). VideaHealth ham o'z mahsulotini "the first ambient AI scribe purpose-built for dentistry" deb sotadi ([BusinessWire](https://secure.businesswire.com/news/home/20251029535866/en/VideaHealth-Launches-Voice-Notes-the-First-Ambient-AI-Scribe-Purpose-Built-for-Dentistry)). Bu HS1'ning "industry-first" da'vosiga zid, ikkalasi ham marketing. Bola AI Voice Perio Open Dental, Dentrix, Eaglesoft va Curve bilan integratsiyalashgan. Vendor 99% aniqlik va gigiyenistga kuniga 18,5 daqiqa tejashni da'vo qiladi ([Bola](https://bola.ai/solutions/voice-perio/)). Rentgen AI (Pearl, Overjet, Videa) **rangli overlay** ko'rinishida chiqadi va bemorga tushuntirish uchun vizual material vazifasini ham bajaradi ([Pearl newsroom](https://hellopearl.com/news/pearl-launches-second-opinion-3d-bringing-fda-cleared-3d-imaging-ai-to-general-dentists-for-the-first-time)). Barcha mahsulotlarda ishonch modeli bir xil: AI qoralama tayyorlaydi, klinitsist ko'rib chiqib qo'shadi.

Front-desk dashboard ham shu yo'nalishda. Denticon'ning Morning Huddle paneli (2023) ishlab chiqarish maqsadlarini ko'rsatadi ([Planet DDS](https://planetdds.com/resource-library/morning-huddle-dashboard-within-denticon-start-your-day-with-insights)). 2026-yilda Dentrix Ascend "Ask" funksiyasini taqdim etdi: istalgan xodim oddiy tilda so'raydi, masalan "Who has unverified insurance this week?". Ascend o'zining MCP qatlamini ham ochdi ([HS1](https://www.henryscheinone.com/about-us/press-release/dentrix-ascend-opens-mcp-layer/)). DentalOS esa ochiqchasiga "persona-driven", ya'ni har bir rol uchun alohida tajriba yo'nalishini e'lon qilgan. Billingdagi AI to'lqini (sug'urta tekshiruvi, Curve Pay, Eligibility+) asosan AQSh sug'urta tizimiga xos ([Curve blog](https://curvedental.com/blog/thankful-for-curve-users)). O'zbekiston uchun undan uchta naqsh foydali: statusga bog'langan hisob-kitob, o'rnatilgan to'lov va avtomatik solishtirish, a'zolik rejalari.

Dark mode bu segmentda hali kam uchraydi. Dentally 2025-yil iyulda hamjamiyatdan dark mode'ga talab bormi deb so'radi ([Dentally community](https://community.dentally.com/dentally-vision-43/dark-mode-graphics-347)). Veterinariya dasturi Shepherd esa 2025-yilda "first PMS with dark mode" deb da'vo qildi ([Shepherd blog](https://www.shepherd.vet/blog/welcome-to-dark-mode-your-eyes-will-thank-you/)). Bemor tomonida integratsiya chuqurlashmoqda: Jane mijoz ilovasi va xavfsiz yozishmalarni chiqardi ([Jane press release](https://tools.prnewswire.com/en-us/live/20823/release/20251119EN27859)), Dentrix Ascend esa hujjat suratlaridan AI yordamida ma'lumot oladigan raqamli anketalarni ([BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260309270533/en/)).

Laboratoriya va klinika almashinuvida etalon model bitta **umumiy case obyekti va uning holatlar mashinasi**. Medit Link'da klinika buyurtmani "Order Box"ga yuboradi. Holatlar: Pending → Accepted → Shipped → Case Completed, shuningdek Rejected va Canceled. Laboratoriya qabul qilmaguncha klinika buyurtmani bekor qila oladi, oxirida klinika "Confirm Delivery" tugmasini bosadi ([Medit Order Box](https://medit.document360.io/docs/order-boxin-box)). Buyurtma holati va ishlab chiqarish holati alohida yuritiladi ([Medit Case Box/Work Box](https://medit.document360.io/docs/case-boxwork-box)). 3Shape Unite skanerlashda o'lchangan rangni (shade) buyurtmaga avtomatik biriktiradi, ya'ni rang erkin matn emas, tuzilgan maydon ([3Shape Unite 23.1](https://support.3shape.com/en_US/3shape-unite-23-how-to/whats-new-in-unite-231)). Dandy'da shifokor laboratoriya bilan chat yuritadi, raqamli wax-up'ni tasdiqlaydi, qayta tayyorlashni (remake) alohida amal sifatida yuboradi ([Dandy portal](https://www.meetdandy.com/technology/portal-and-mobile-app/)). Bularning hammasi vendor hujjatlari. MDH'da klinika↔laboratoriya tarmog'i topilmadi. Laboratoriyalar AverSoft "ЗТЛ" kabi alohida dasturlar ([a2is.ru](https://a2is.ru/catalog/programmy-upravleniya-stomatologiyey/aversoft)) yoki Planfix kabi umumiy vositalardan foydalanadi ([Planfix](https://planfix.com/industry-solutions/dental-lab-clinic-orders/)).

## Foydalanuvchilar tezlik va o'rganish osonligini qadrlaydi

### Sharhlarda shikoyatlar mahsulot oilasiga qarab takrorlanadi

Dalil cheklovini oldindan aytish kerak. Reddit'ga to'g'ridan-to'g'ri kirib bo'lmadi. Capterra, G2, GetApp, Software Advice va TrustRadius 403 xatosini qaytardi, shuning uchun bu saytlardan olingan iqtiboslar **qidiruv natijalari parchalariga** asoslangan. Matn asl nusxaga yaqin, lekin to'liq sharh bilan solishtirilmagan. Sana va muallif roli ko'pincha ko'rinmaydi. Sanasi bilan olingan iqtiboslar faqat Softwarefinder'dan. Yulduzli reytinglar 4,1–4,8 oralig'ida to'planib qolgan va mahsulotlarni deyarli ajratmaydi, shuning uchun foydali signal "kamchiliklar" maydonida takrorlanuvchi mavzular. Mavzularning necha marta uchrashi bo'yicha mustaqil sanoq yo'q.

Eng ko'p takrorlangan shikoyat **sekinlik**, va uning ma'nosi arxitekturaga bog'liq. Bulutli tizimlarda sekinlik internet sifati, rasmlar hajmi va eng gavjum soatlarga bog'liq. Dentrix Ascend haqida "the response time can be slow, especially the imaging" deyilgan ([Capterra Ascend](https://capterra.com/p/177828/Dentrix-Ascend/reviews/), parcha). tab32 "slows down tremendously at 3 pm" ([G2 tab32](https://www.g2.com/products/tab32/reviews), parcha). Curve uchun ekstremal holat ham bor: "just opening a profile can take 10 minutes" (2025-yil oktabr, istisno holat) ([Softwarefinder Curve](https://softwarefinder.com/emr-software/curve-dental/reviews)). Server tizimlarida esa sabab apparat va texnik xizmat: Eaglesoft "can run slow and occasionally crash during busy practice hours" ([SelectHub](https://www.selecthub.com/dental-software/eaglesoft/)).

Ikkinchi shikoyat **ortiqcha kliklar va qatlamli ekranlar**: "many instances where you would expect one click … instead it takes two or three clicks" ([Capterra Dentrix](https://www.capterra.com/p/2329/Dentrix/reviews/), asosan 2019–2022, eski). Yangiroq misol Ascend front-ofis xodimidan: "a lot of extra steps for what I do". Bu tizim har bir xizmat (rasm, yozuv, ko'rik, parodont xaritasi) uchun foydalanuvchi nomini qayta tanlashni talab qiladi ([Capterra Ascend p.5](https://www.capterra.com/p/177828/Dentrix-Ascend/reviews/?page=5)). Uchinchi shikoyat **ish vaqtidagi yangilanishlar**, bulutda ham, desktopda ham: "updates happen during operation hours and it can cause a major slow down or halt to our day" ([Capterra Ascend](https://capterra.com/p/177828/Dentrix-Ascend/reviews/)). Curve haqida ham shunday: "some updates take a while to roll out during business hours" ([Softwarefinder Curve](https://softwarefinder.com/emr-software/curve-dental/reviews)). To'rtinchi shikoyat **hisobot va billing chalkashligi**. U deyarli har bir mahsulot oilasida bor: Ascend uchun "the reports are way too confusing" ([Capterra Ascend](https://capterra.com/p/177828/Dentrix-Ascend/reviews/)), Curve uchun "A/R and billing … could definitely be improved" ([Softwarefinder Curve](https://softwarefinder.com/emr-software/curve-dental/reviews)), shuning uchun bu bitta vendor emas, butun kategoriya bo'shlig'i. Bulut uzilishida Curve foydalanuvchisi "no access to patient notes or medical history" deb yozgan ([Softwarefinder Curve](https://softwarefinder.com/emr-software/curve-dental/reviews)). IT-provayder 30 daqiqalik internet uzilishi jadval, billing va charting'ni to'xtatishini aytadi ([Siotek](https://siotek.net/resources/dental-practice-management-software-comparison), tijoriy manba). Ko'rib chiqilgan bulutli mahsulotlarning birortasida haqiqiy oflayn rejim topilmadi.

Maqtovlarda xom tezlik kam tilga olinadi, ko'proq **o'rganish osonligi** maqtaladi. Ascend "user friendly and easy to teach to staff", Eaglesoft "the most user friendly and easiest to learn", Dentally "so easy to use" deb ta'riflanadi ([Capterra Eaglesoft](https://www.capterra.com/p/60064/EagleSoft-13-00/reviews/); [Capterra Dentally](https://www.capterra.com.au/reviews/153816/dentally)). Curve haqida "several quick ways to pull up patient charts" deyilgan. Open Dental'ni Dentrix va Eaglesoft'dan o'tgan foydalanuvchi "simple to learn and very customizable" deb atagan va ma'lumot egaligini qadrlagan ([Capterra Open Dental](https://www.capterra.com/p/122350/Open-Dental/reviews/), qidiruv xulosasi). Jane va Cliniko kabi fizioterapiya vositalari sayqal va soddalik bilan yutadi ([GetApp](https://www.getapp.com/healthcare-pharmaceuticals-software/a/jane-app/compare/cliniko/)). Bu kichik klinikalarda aniq qarorlar qabul qilingan, toza UX funksiya chuqurligidan ustun kelishini ko'rsatadi.

### Har rol boshqa xarajatni his qiladi

Front desk **o'tkazuvchanlik** xarajatini his qiladi: ro'yxatga olish va to'lovdagi qadamlar soni, yangilanish va uzilish tufayli to'xtashlar, billing murakkabligi. Klinitsistlar (gigiyenist, assistent) **kontekst** xarajatini his qiladi: har amalda o'zini qayta tanlash, qo'lqop bilan kreslo yonida navigatsiya, "Only one person could work in the chart at a time" ([Capterra Eaglesoft](https://www.capterra.com/p/60064/EagleSoft-13-00/reviews/)). Curve'da "The biggest issue from the clinical side is the charting" (2025-yil avgust) ([Softwarefinder Curve](https://softwarefinder.com/emr-software/curve-dental/reviews)). Egalar va menejerlar **iqtisodiy** xarajatni ko'radi: qo'shimcha funksiyalar uchun alohida to'lov, qulflanish (lock-in), qo'llab-quvvatlash soatlari, o'qitish narxi, ko'p filial. Dentrix uchun "training model makes scaling nearly impossible" iqtibosi bor ([Dentaltown/RevUp](https://www.dentaltown.com/channel/post/25865/what-dentists-are-saying-about-practice-management-software), tijoriy post). Muallif roli kam ko'ringani uchun bu rol bo'yicha taqsimot bir nechta sharhga tayanadi. Shuni ham hisobga olish kerak: bozor yetakchisini biladigan xodimni yollash oson. Yangi mahsulot bu ustunlikni faqat juda tez o'rganiladigan interfeys bilan qoplashi mumkin.

### Ilmiy dalil bu shikoyatlarni xavfsizlik va burnout masalasiga aylantiradi

Melnick va boshq. (2020, Mayo Clin Proc) 870 AQSh shifokori orasida EHR'ning o'rtacha **SUS ballini 45,9 ± 21,9** deb topdi. Bu avvalgi tadqiqotlardagi SUS ballarining eng past 9% iga to'g'ri keladi. **Har bir qo'shimcha SUS ball burnout ehtimolining 3% pastligi bilan bog'liq (OR 0,97)**, munosabat dozaga bog'liq ([PubMed 31735343](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31735343/), tadqiqot, o'z-o'zini baholash). Arndt va boshq. (2017) Epic loglaridan har ish kuni **5,9 soat** EHR vaqti hisobladi. Uning taxminan 44% i kotiblik va ma'muriy ish, 24% i inbox ([Ann Fam Med](https://www.annfammed.org/content/15/5/419)). 2024-yilgi davomi bu vaqt 2023-yilgacha o'sishda davom etganini ko'rsatdi ([Ann Fam Med 2024](https://www.annfammed.org/content/22/1/12)). Sinsky va boshq. (2016) shifokor kunining **49,2% i EHR va stol ishiga, 27% i bemor bilan yuzma-yuz muloqotga** ketishini aniqladi ([Healio](https://www.healio.com/primary-care/practice-management/news/online/%7B063320c8-6954-45b8-89dc-3b293772d441%7D/physicians-spend-nearly-50-of-their-time-on-ehr-desk-work)). Usability xavfsizlik bilan ham bog'liq: pediatriya bo'yicha 9 000 ta xavfsizlik hisobotining **36% i** EHR usability muammolari bilan bog'langan ([HCInnovation](https://www.hcinnovationgroup.com/clinical-it/news/13030882/ehr-usability-issues-impact-pediatric-patient-safety-research-finds)).

Stomatologiyaga xos dalil yanada aniq. Thyvalikakath va boshq. (2008, JADA) Dentrix, EagleSoft, SoftDent va PracticeWorks'ning charting modullarini yangi foydalanuvchilar bilan sinadi. Natija: **to'g'ri bajarilish 16–64%, noto'g'ri 18–38%**, jami **286 usability muammosi** ([PMC2736678](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2736678), abstrakt darajasida, eski lekin asosiy tadqiqot). Shu tizimlarning evristik tahlilida eng ko'p buzilgan tamoyillar "Consistency and standards", "Match between system and real world" va "Error prevention" bo'lib chiqdi ([ProQuest listing](https://scholars.proquest.com/gallery/indiana/profiles/B03F8595AC1BA51A1E0C70CD511BC8F7/publications?page=3), ikkilamchi). Walji, Kalenderian va boshq. (2013) axiUm'da tashxis kiritishni sinadi. Bitta tashxisda muvaffaqiyat **41%**, besh tashxisli rejada **0%**, SUS **56,9**. Muvaffaqiyatli vazifa ekspert etaloni ~54 soniya o'rniga 3 daqiqa 8 soniyada bajarildi. Aniqlangan sabablar: atamalar nomi emas, kodi bo'yicha saralangan; bir marta va ikki marta bosish natijani o'zgartiradi; qo'shish tugmasi "Update" deb noto'g'ri nomlangan; tuzilgan kiritishni erkin matn bilan chetlab o'tish mumkin; sinonim qidiruvi yo'q ([PMC3492538](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3492538), tadqiqot, to'liq o'qilgan). Talabalarning sensorli iTooth loyihasida ishtirokchilarning **44% i charting statuslaridan chalkashgan** ([Marshfield SRIP](https://www.marshfieldresearch.org/srip/students/adam-sorenson), peer review qilinmagan). Usul bo'yicha xulosa: foydalanuvchi testi muammolarning 83% ini topgan, evristik tahlil esa user testing topganlarining faqat yarmini.

Ogohlantirish charchog'i (alert fatigue) eng ko'p o'rganilgan mavzu. Dori-allergiya ogohlantirishlari **43,7–97%** hollarda e'tiborsiz qoldiriladi. Asosiy sabab "bemor bu dorini avval ko'targan" ([PubMed 34990941](https://www.pubmed.gov/34990941/), tizimli sharh). E'tiborsiz qoldirilgan allergiya ogohlantirishlarining **63,4–100%** i klinik jihatdan to'g'ri qaror bo'lgan ([PMC7400042](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC7400042/)). Paterno va boshq. (2009) bir xil bilim bazasidan foydalangan ikki shifoxonani solishtirdi. Ogohlantirishlarni darajalarga ajratgan shifoxonada eng jiddiy ogohlantirishlarni qabul qilish **100%**, ajratmaganida **34%** bo'ldi ([JAMIA](https://academic.oup.com/jamia/article/16/1/40/865495)). Boshqa bir tadqiqotda tijoriy EHR'ga o'tgandan keyin bu ko'rsatkich **100% dan 8,4% ga** tushgan, mualliflar buni ogohlantirishlar yuklamasi va taqdimoti o'zgarishi bilan izohlaydi ([Springer](https://rd.springer.com/content/pdf/10.1007/s11606-018-4415-9.pdf), ikkilamchi). Stomatologiyada qog'oz va raqamli kartani solishtirgan tadqiqotda kartani ochganda ajratib ko'rsatilgan jiddiy patologiya xabarini barcha talabalar payqagan. Shu manba AQSh kogortasini keltiradi: diabetli bemorlarning **15,1%** i va gipertoniyali bemorlarning **29%** i bu kasalligini stomatologga aytmagan ([PMC12745653](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC12745653/)). Demak muammo faqat ogohlantirishni ko'rsatishda emas. Tibbiy anamnez ma'lumoti har tashrifda osongina yangilanib turishi kerak.

Kognitiv yuklama bo'yicha eng kuchli eksperimental dalil Mayo'ning AWARE tadqiqoti. Ixchamlashtirilgan va muhimlik bo'yicha tartiblangan bemor xulosasi interfeysi standart EHR'ga nisbatan vazifa yuklamasini va kognitiv xatolarni kamaytirgan ([PMC3632280](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3632280), ICU, effekt hajmi olinmagan). Stomatologiyada tablar va bitta sahifani to'g'ridan-to'g'ri solishtirgan nazoratli tadqiqot **topilmadi**. AI scribe'lar bo'yicha RCT natijalari kamtar. Lukac va boshq. (2025, NEJM AI) Nabla uchun yozuvga ketgan vaqtning **−9,5%** kamayishini topdi, DAX uchun farq ahamiyatsiz (−1,7%) ([PubMed 41497288](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41497288/)). Nazorat guruhisiz pre/post tadqiqotda burnout **51,9% dan 38,8% ga** tushgan ([PubMed 41037268](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41037268/)). Aniqlikda esa xavf bor: UC Davis auditida yozuvlarning **18%** ida tushib qolgan ma'lumot, **11,5%** ida gallyutsinatsiya, **5,3%** ida jiddiy xavfli xato topilgan ([JMIR Med Inform 2026](https://medinform.jmir.org/2026/1/e86474)). Kreslo yonidagi cheklovlar ham hujjatlangan. Klinika klaviaturalari patogen organizmlar manbai bo'lib qolgan va himoya plyonkasi yoki dezinfeksiya talab qiladi ([Dimensions of Dental Hygiene](https://dimensionsofdentalhygiene.com/article/contaminated-surfaces/)). Qo'lqop bilan sensorli ekranga birinchi urinishdagi aniqlik pasayadi. **≥15 mm** tegish zonasi va 3 mm oraliq bo'lganda xato tugma bosilmagan (n=6, harbiy kontekst) ([CAS katalog](https://katalog.lib.cas.cz/KNAV/EdsRecord/edsair,edsair.doi...........9465c900e5780072de7a168d106fd14d)). ## Dalil chegaralari va noaniqliklar

Bu hisobotdagi dalillarning og'irligi bir xil emas. Eng kuchli qism klinik usability bo'yicha peer-reviewed tadqiqotlar (Melnick, Walji, Thyvalikakath, Paterno, Lukac). Lekin ularning ko'pi AQSh shifoxona EHR'lari haqida, stomatologiyaga xoslari esa 2008–2013 yillarga oid. Trend bo'limidagi deyarli barcha da'volar vendor press-relizlari va yordam sahifalaridan olingan, natija raqamlari ("99% aniqlik", "50% kam charting") tekshirilmagan marketing. NexHealth bir xil ko'rsatkichni bir sahifada 73% ([NexHealth Scheduling](https://www.nexhealth.com/features/scheduling)), boshqasida 43% ([NexHealth Online Payments](https://www.nexhealth.com/features/online-payments)) deb keltiradi. G2 va Capterra iqtiboslari qidiruv parchalaridan olingan. Stomatologiya subredditlari (r/Dentistry, r/dentalassistant va boshqalar) o'qib bo'lmadi, faqat bitta r/Uzbekistan muhokamasi mirror orqali o'qildi. MDH iqtiboslarining hammasi vendorlar javob yozadigan bitta saytdan (crmindex.ru). O'zbekiston bo'yicha klinikalar dasturiy ta'minot ulushi, viloyatlarda internet ishonchliligi, bot orqali yozilish konversiyasi va Telegram eslatmalari samarasi haqida hech qanday ma'lumot topilmadi. Stomatologiyada tablar va bitta sahifani solishtirgan, odontogramma interaksiya uslublarini taqqoslagan yoki ikki tomonlama tasdiqlashni sinagan nazoratli tadqiqot ham yo'q.

## Xulosa

Dental OS uchun asosiy xulosa: yangi tartib ixtiro qilishga hojat yo'q. Kalendar, status va kutish zali konvensiyalari barqaror, foydalanuvchilar ham ularni kutadi. Ustunlik raqobatchilar bo'sh qoldirgan uch joyda olinadi. Birinchisi, administrator ish joyining "ishonchliligi": holat yo'qolmasligi, qidiruv telefon bo'yicha ishlashi, Telegram so'rovlari bir joyda bo'lishi, yangilanish ish vaqtini buzmasligi, internetsiz ham ishlash. Ikkinchisi, ogohlantirishlarning darajaga ajratilishi. Bu "Bugun"dagi e'tibor paneliga ham, kartadagi allergiya banneriga ham taalluqli, chunki bir xil bilim bazasida ham taqdimot va yuklama o'zgarganda jiddiy ogohlantirishni qabul qilish 8,4% gacha tushgani qayd etilgan. Uchinchisi, laboratoriya buyurtmasining tuzilgan maydonlari. Raqobatchi bu yerda dastur emas, qog'oz va telefon. AI to'lqini AQSh bozorida dolzarb, lekin hozircha uz va ru tilida aniqlik dalili yo'q va xato ulushi sezilarli. Shuning uchun O'zbekiston uchun AI'dan ko'ra Telegram bot, oflayn ishonchlilik va fiskal kassa tezroq foyda beradi.

Kutilmagan, lekin amaliy topilma: Planet DDS 2026-yil iyulda DSO'lar uchun o'z platformasini aynan **"DentalOS"** nomi bilan ishga tushirdi ([Planet DDS](https://www.planetdds.com/newsroom/dentalos-enterprise-platform/)). Bu huquqiy xulosa emas. Lekin mahsulot nomi sifatida "Dental OS" qidiruvda va hamkorlar bilan muloqotda AQSh mahsuloti bilan aralashib ketishi mumkin. Shuning uchun nomni bozorga chiqishdan oldin qayta ko'rib chiqish yoki unga mahalliy farqlovchi qo'shish arziydi.
